How to use the timeline

Read this chronology as the project's emergence, not only as a date list.

The most useful way to browse here is to follow how mission signals, research outputs, releases, community invitations, and site milestones start to converge into one coherent platform identity.

Start with featured milestones

These entries show the clearest turning points in public visibility.

They reveal when the platform's narrative, production layer, and public channels became easier to recognize as one project.

Then fill in the surrounding signals

The standard entries add density, continuity, and historical texture.

They help explain how the flagship moments were supported by smaller releases, dialogues, and archive traces.

Keep moving across layers

Every milestone can lead outward into productions, research records, archive traces, and community calls.

This is what turns the chronology into a navigable field instead of a closed timeline widget.
