# Phase 6A Content Elevation Plan

## Goal
Strengthen the site's writing so it feels authored, serious, and culturally resonant while preserving evidence integrity and the structured content model.

## Core Content Problem
The site now explains itself clearly, but it does not yet speak with enough force. Too much copy still sounds like archive metadata, route description, or responsible summary language. That is useful, but the public-facing pages need more narrative conviction.

## Content Priorities by Page

### Homepage
Needs major rewriting.

What should change:
- stronger opening statement
- sharper articulation of what Atyaf Al Ard is and why it matters
- clearer bridge between evidence, memory, cinema, and public history
- more persuasive invitation to browse, watch, read, and support

Needs expansion:
- hero headline and subhead
- one short manifesto-style identity block
- one “why this platform exists now” section
- stronger featured section intros

What should stay concise:
- stats
- CTA labels
- section eyebrows

### About
Needs major rewriting and expansion.

What should change:
- move from brief description to institutional-quality project statement
- explain origin, ambition, method, and public value
- better articulate why the project combines research, storytelling, and media

Needs expansion:
- mission
- method
- public promise
- near-term build logic
- long-term vision

### Productions
Needs moderate rewriting.

What should change:
- page intro should frame productions as a cinematic and educational output layer, not only a filterable list
- flagship productions need richer copy and more distinct framing

Needs expansion:
- flagship intros
- type explanations
- possibly one curator note for the productions section

What should stay concise:
- standard card summaries for secondary works

### Research
Needs moderate-to-major rewriting.

What should change:
- reposition from “research archive” to “public knowledge layer”
- clarify which entries are foundational, which are evidence briefs, and which are public-facing learning tools

Needs expansion:
- section intro
- foundational reading framing
- evidence brief intros
- document explanation copy around PDFs and text copies

### Timeline
Needs moderate rewriting.

What should change:
- frame the timeline as a project evolution narrative, not only a date list
- strengthen milestone descriptions where they are too procedural

What should stay concise:
- individual milestone summaries unless the event is truly flagship

### Archive
Needs selective rewriting.

What should change:
- clearer framing of what the archive represents
- clearer distinction between recovered signals, public posts, interviews, and community calls
- more elegant handling of partial records and manual-review cases

What should stay concise:
- standard post summaries for low-confidence or thin-caption items

### Community / Support / Contact
Needs major rewriting.

What should change:
- these pages must feel like genuine invitations into the project
- support needs a stronger case for contribution
- contact needs more confidence, not just channel links
- community should explain participation, not only list calls

## Content Types That Need the Most Attention

### High priority
- `content/site/home.json`
- `content/site/about.json`
- `content/site/support.json`
- `content/site/contact.json`
- flagship production records
- foundational research records

### Medium priority
- community/support call summaries
- timeline milestone intros
- archive featured summaries

### Lower priority
- secondary launch-window posts with partial evidence
- low-signal or repetitive records that should remain concise

## Writing Modes to Use Later

### Narrative / atmospheric writing
Use for:
- homepage
- about
- flagship productions
- section intros

Tone:
- serious
- cinematic
- rooted
- confident
- not inflated

### Factual / reference writing
Use for:
- evidence blocks
- archive details
- source panels
- confidence labels
- partial-caption disclosures

Tone:
- transparent
- exact
- calm
- source-aware

### Invitation / participation writing
Use for:
- support
- community
- contact

Tone:
- open
- credible
- specific about what help is needed
- not needy or vague

## Content Expansion Recommendations

### Expand strongly
- homepage identity section
- about mission and method
- support case for backing the project
- flagship production descriptions
- research framing around PDFs, evidence, and learning

### Expand selectively
- timeline milestones
- archive featured records
- community/support call detail pages

### Keep concise
- repetitive launch-window posts
- low-confidence archive items
- utility labels and metadata

## Bilingual Content Guidance
- Arabic should not be treated as a direct mirror only.
- English and Arabic should feel equally authored, not one primary and one translated shell.
- Arabic display copy should be tuned for rhythm and dignity, not merely completeness.
- Evidence-facing text can remain closer to source structure; public-facing intros should be better shaped.

## Execution Recommendation
Content should not be rewritten all at once.

Best order:
1. homepage
2. about
3. support and contact
4. flagship productions and foundational research
5. section intros for archive, timeline, productions, research, community
6. selective refinement of secondary records
