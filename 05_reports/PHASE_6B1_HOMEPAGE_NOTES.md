# Phase 6B-1 Homepage Notes

## Homepage Intent
The homepage was upgraded from an archive-forward dashboard into a stronger cultural arrival experience. The goal was to make the first screen feel like the opening frame of a serious Palestinian project rather than a neutral content index.

## Key Homepage Decisions
- Put the project thesis directly in the hero instead of relying on generic platform language.
- Use the existing hero video and still image in a more composed stacked layout, rather than treating them as simple adjacent media.
- Lead with a stronger emotional and editorial voice without inventing new facts.
- Make the homepage explain how to enter the site, not just what content types exist.
- Keep the research layer visibly important so the site does not drift into being read as media-only.
- Keep support/community on the homepage, but position it as a live-building signal rather than a donation-first block.

## English Copy Direction
- More declarative and confident.
- Less “archive foundation” language.
- Stronger emphasis on public memory, evidence, and cinematic storytelling.
- Better pacing between thesis, explanation, and browse actions.

## Arabic Copy Direction
- Stronger rhetorical weight in headings.
- Less literal mirroring of older English phrasing.
- Better dignity in section framing and action labels.
- Preserved clarity and public legibility while making the tone feel more serious and grounded.

## Homepage Structure After This Pass
1. Cinematic hero with thesis, signals, stats, and media
2. Project positioning section
3. Route-entry browse section
4. Public method / evidence section
5. Flagship productions
6. Foundational research
7. Timeline of emergence
8. Archive signals
9. Support and community

## What Still Needs Future Homepage Work

- stronger motion choreography once the section pages are upgraded
- deeper footer/header polish
- more refined evidence-source presentation blocks
- tighter mobile fine-tuning after wider site redesign phases
