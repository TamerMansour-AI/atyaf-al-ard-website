## Production Principles Used
- Keep the palette warm, earth-toned, and editorial.
- Use line-based symbolic imagery instead of random illustrative scenes.
- Favor SVG for speed, coherence, and easy reuse.
- Keep all assets language-neutral where possible so English and Arabic pages can share them cleanly.

## Intentionally Not Produced
- Large batches of AI-generated scene art.
- Random page-by-page decorative illustrations.
- A mixed icon pack from multiple outside sources.
- Heavy PNG/JPG hero replacements that would increase page weight without improving coherence.
