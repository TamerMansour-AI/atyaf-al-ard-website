# Phase 6A Master Plan for Design and Content Elevation

## Mission
Elevate the current structured site into a more premium, cinematic, emotionally resonant, and content-rich public platform without discarding the existing archive structure.

This phase is planning only. It defines the target, the order of work, and the assets/content that should be produced later.

## What Is Currently Weak
- The homepage is clear but not commanding.
- The visual system is safe and repetitive.
- Typography is generic and undercuts the project's seriousness.
- Arabic is structurally mirrored but not visually elevated.
- The archive and research layers are stronger functionally than emotionally.
- Support and contact remain too thin for a project of this scope.

## Target Experience

### What the site should feel like
- cinematic but disciplined
- culturally rooted, not ornamental
- research-driven but emotionally alive
- archival, editorial, and contemporary at once

### Homepage target impression
The homepage should feel like entering a living Palestinian knowledge-and-storytelling world, not opening a neat archive index. It should signal seriousness, memory, evidence, artistic ambition, and long-term platform intent within the first screen.

### Archive / research / production target feel
- Productions should feel like a curated cinematic catalog.
- Research should feel like an evidence and interpretation lab.
- Archive should feel like a recovered memory field with traceable source depth.
- Timeline should feel like project evolution, not only dated cards.

## Design System Direction

### Palette direction
- replace the mostly flat beige system with a deeper earth-led palette
- keep warmth, but add contrast through charcoal, clay, ash, stone, and ember tones
- use one restrained luminous accent, not multiple bright accents

### Typography direction
- move away from generic product typography
- use one expressive display face for English headlines
- use a more intentional Arabic display/text pairing
- keep body copy highly readable, but with more editorial character

### Component direction
- stop treating all content as the same rounded card
- introduce clearer component families:
- hero compositions
- editorial panels
- evidence/source modules
- flagship cards
- standard archive cards
- timeline milestone blocks

### Card style direction
- less "soft SaaS card"
- more layered editorial surfaces, stronger hierarchy, and more differentiated emphasis

### Icon style direction
- restrained line icons or symbolic markers
- evidence-led, not playful
- consistent across filters, source labels, and metadata

### Image style direction
- cinematic, textural, restrained
- no random collage of unrelated visuals
- every image family should feel part of one authored world

### Motion and video direction
- keep motion limited and meaningful
- homepage hero can carry the strongest motion moment
- subtle reveal or parallax can help later, but motion should never compensate for weak composition

### PDFs and evidence panels
- these should remain important
- elevate them visually with better document framing, source blocks, and reading context
- avoid making source panels look like generic utility widgets

## Content Strategy

### Pages needing major rewriting
- homepage
- about
- support
- contact

### Pages needing moderate rewriting
- productions index
- research index
- archive index
- timeline index
- community

### Pages needing selective enrichment
- flagship production detail pages
- foundational research detail pages
- key media/archive detail pages

### Writing split
- narrative writing for homepage/about/flagship intros
- factual writing for evidence blocks and archive detail layers
- invitation writing for support/community/contact

## Asset Strategy Summary

### Highest-priority assets to prepare later
1. homepage hero art system
2. section anchor visuals
3. flagship production cover treatments
4. evidence/document presentation assets
5. coherent icon family

### Best use of Freepik / Nano Banana 2 later
- concepting hero visuals
- creating section anchor imagery
- generating refined symbolic or atmospheric visuals
- building a coherent cinematic asset family

### What should not happen
- no bulk generation of random cultural imagery
- no replacing evidence visuals with synthetic art
- no mixing multiple incompatible styles

## Execution Order

### First
Build the upgraded design system and visual grammar.

Reason:
- this prevents rewriting pages twice
- it gives the whole site a stronger base before content polish

### Second
Elevate the homepage.

Reason:
- it is the most visible weak point
- it sets the tone for everything else

### Third
Upgrade the section index pages:
- productions
- research
- timeline
- archive
- community
- support
- contact

Reason:
- these pages define most of the public browsing experience

### Fourth
Upgrade flagship detail pages.

Reason:
- the best content should feel truly premium once users click in

### Fifth
Perform Arabic and bilingual refinement.

Reason:
- the site already mirrors language routes, but the polish gap still needs focused work

### Sixth
Integrate final asset generation and polish.

Reason:
- generated assets should support a settled system, not define it too early

## What to Postpone
- broad motion polish
- secondary decorative assets
- exhaustive rewriting of low-signal archive records
- advanced experimental interactions
- any rebuild that would disrupt the structured archive model

## Risks
- over-design that competes with evidence readability
- generic cinematic visuals from image generation
- Arabic layouts becoming cramped under a denser design system
- too many new assets without a governing art direction
- section inconsistency if pages are redesigned one by one without a shared system

## Recommendation
Do not rebuild the site in one pass.

Use several controlled phases:
1. visual system
2. homepage
3. section indices
4. flagship details
5. bilingual refinement
6. asset infusion
7. QA

This is the safest path to achieve a major elevation without losing the structured progress already built.
