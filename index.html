What is Atyaf Al Ard?

Atyaf Al Ard is not a single film, but a long-term, evolving project. It combines evidence-based research with cinematic media and AI tools to tell a deeper Palestinian story — one that is rooted in the land, in continuity, and in everyday life, not in shallow emotional spectacle.

Three streams of work

Where research, media, and youth meet

Research

Studies on genetic continuity, archaeology, landscape history, and social life in Palestine. Each project is documented, sourced, and shared through interactive spaces such as NotebookLM.

Media

Short films, visual sequences, and music inspired by the research. AI is used as a creative partner in image, sound, and editing — always grounded in real history and lived experience.

Youth & Platforms

Training a youth team to run the project’s social platforms, assist in research, and experiment with AI tools. The goal is to turn Atyaf Al Ard into a living lab, not just a finished product.

Current focus

Phase 1: building the foundations

The first phase of Atyaf Al Ard is about building a solid base — scientifically, visually, and socially. This is what we are working on right now:

  • Genetic continuity research linking ancient Levantine populations and today’s Palestinians.
  • Visual and musical pieces that explore Canaanite and Palestinian history through different eras.
  • Public research spaces on NotebookLM where people can explore sources, questions, and summaries.
  • Designing the project’s identity, website, and the first training path for a youth media & research team.
