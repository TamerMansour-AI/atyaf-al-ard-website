What this section does

The community layer makes the project feel inhabited, not only authored.

These records show how the platform has asked for help, opened discussion channels, and framed public involvement while it is still being built.

Volunteer energy

Some calls are about immediate platform-building needs and media coordination.

These should feel practical and alive, not tucked away as minor archive leftovers.

Shared following

Community channels matter because they create continuity around releases and discussion.

This helps the site present the project as an evolving public platform rather than a one-way showcase.

Cross-linked participation

Community calls are strongest when they stay linked to the archive and timeline layers.

That relationship makes the outreach feel grounded in actual moments rather than generic calls for help.
