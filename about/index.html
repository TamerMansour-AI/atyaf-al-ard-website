About Atyaf Al Ard

Atyaf Al Ard is envisioned as a space to collect, connect, and care for Palestinian stories across time.

Our mission is to ground creative work in rigorous research, honoring community knowledge and lived experiences.

This project exists to offer a living archive, inviting collaborators to shape narratives with dignity, nuance, and imagination.
