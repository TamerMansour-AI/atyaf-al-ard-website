Project updates

Here we share milestones, research releases, and collaboration calls from Atyaf Al Ard. This is a lightweight log of how the project is growing over time.

December 2025 Launch

Atyaf Al Ard website is now live

We have launched the first public version of the Atyaf Al Ard website as a home for our evidence-first work about Palestine. The site now includes a bilingual overview of the project, a media page with our first films, and a research section with foundational visual maps and NotebookLM spaces.

This version is intentionally simple: it focuses on clarity, trust, and giving people a place to start exploring. Over time, we will add more research tracks, films, and learning tools.

December 2025 Call for team

Call for volunteers and future team members

In this first phase, Atyaf Al Ard is looking for volunteers and emerging collaborators who believe in evidence-first storytelling about Palestine and want to help it grow. We are especially interested in young people who can help with social media, content adaptation, basic research, and community outreach.

At this moment, roles are volunteer-based until we secure funding, but our intention is clear: to build a small core team that can be compensated fairly once support arrives. If you feel this project should exist and want to be part of its early stages, we would be happy to hear from you.
