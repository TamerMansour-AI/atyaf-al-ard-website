Support & Collaborate with Atyaf Al Ard

Atyaf Al Ard is an independent, long-term Palestinian initiative. We work at the intersection of archaeology, genetics, daily-life heritage, and visual storytelling to show Palestine as a continuous human landscape — not just a sequence of conflicts. Your support helps us turn deep research into films, visual archives, music, and learning tools that can live beyond social media trends and political cycles.

What your support makes possible in Phase 1

Research & evidence translation

Time to read, analyze, and synthesize scientific work (genetics, archaeology, anthropology) into accessible narratives that non-experts can understand and share.

Films & visual journeys

Short films, visual essays, and experimental pieces that show Palestine’s deep history through cinematic imagery and sound, not only text or headlines.

Visual maps & infographics

One-page visual frameworks that make complex ideas clear, such as continuity of populations or the evolution of Canaanite culture across time.

Digital archive & website

A living home for videos, research notes, visual maps, and interactive NotebookLM spaces in both Arabic and English, designed to grow over the years.

Youth & emerging team members

Training and mentoring a small group of young collaborators to help with research, social media, and visual production instead of overloading a single person.

Access & translation

Turning Arabic research into bilingual resources so that Palestinians, the region, and the world can all engage with the same evidence rather than competing myths.

Ways to support & collaborate

Grants & institutional funding

For cultural foundations, research centers, and residency programs who want to support long-term, evidence-based storytelling about Palestine.

Examples: multi-month research phases, film production, educational toolkits.

Co-production & media partnerships

For broadcasters, platforms, and studios interested in co-developing films, series, or digital formats grounded in solid research rather than surface-level commentary.

Examples: co-produced documentaries, short series, thematic seasons.

Individual supporters & diaspora

For people who want to contribute smaller amounts or amplify the work through their networks, especially Palestinians and Arabs in the diaspora.

Examples: covering research time, tools, and youth team training.

In-kind and technical support

For tech companies, AI/creative tool providers, and spaces that can offer access, licenses, or infrastructure instead of (or alongside) financial support.

Examples: AI tools, cloud credits, production spaces, post-production support.

How we use support

We treat every form of support — financial, in-kind, or technical — as a responsibility. Funds are used to buy time for deep work (research and writing), to produce films and visual pieces with long-term value, and to train a small team instead of burning out a single person. We do not sell the project’s integrity for quick wins or propaganda.

  • Research & writing time
  • Visual and audio production (images, music, film)
  • Youth team training and fair compensation when funding allows
  • Website development, archiving, and translation

If you represent a foundation, residency, cultural space, media platform, or tech company and want to explore a partnership, share a few lines about who you are and what kind of support you have in mind.
