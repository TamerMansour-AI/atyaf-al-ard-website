This page is intentionally broader than donation language. It frames support as the infrastructure that allows research, archive recovery, production, and public presentation to keep growing together.

What support can mean here

The project needs backing across culture, research, operations, and public presentation.

Support is not limited to cash. This platform also needs time, skilled collaboration, amplification, infrastructure, and sustained belief in the long-form work behind it.

Creative and editorial help

Translation, design, editing, and public-facing polish all matter.

These are the layers that allow the archive and research work to reach people clearly and with dignity.

Research and technical support

Research assistance, GIS support, hosting, and platform-building are part of the same support field.

The site is now structured enough that these forms of help have visible public impact.

Long-term backing

The strongest support is what helps the project keep building without flattening its ambition.

The point is to sustain a serious, bilingual Palestinian platform over time, not only to complete one launch cycle.

A long-term Palestinian research and storytelling platform.
