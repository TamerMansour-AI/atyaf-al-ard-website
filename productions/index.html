How to read this catalog

The production layer is arranged as a story world, not a flat upload list.

Use the flagship works to understand tone and ambition first, then move into the supporting releases that expand atmosphere, historical continuity, and visual language.

Start with the clearest signal

Flagship works show the strongest public articulation of the project.

They carry the best combination of identity, narrative confidence, and broader project recognizability.

Then move into the texture layer

Short fragments, songs, and poetic works build the emotional field around the flagship spine.

These items matter because they turn research themes into recurring motifs and public atmosphere.

Keep the archive connected

Production records are meant to stay linked to research, timeline entries, and source-aware archive traces.

This keeps the cinematic layer grounded in the wider project rather than letting it float as disconnected media.
