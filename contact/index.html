Contact and Public Channels

Until a dedicated inbox is published, the project's public channels are the clearest contact surface. They are also part of how the platform introduces itself, distributes work, and invites people deeper into the archive and research layers.

This page is meant to feel intentional rather than provisional. Even before a dedicated email appears, it should clearly show where to reach the project and what each public channel is best for.

Before you reach out

The strongest contact comes with context.

If you are writing about collaboration, support, translation, research, or media visibility, it helps to indicate which layer of the project you are responding to.

Mention the layer

Say whether you are responding to a production, research item, archive record, or support pathway.

This makes it easier to route the conversation inside a multi-layered project platform.

Name the support type

If you want to help, specify whether the offer is editorial, financial, technical, design, translation, or research support.

Clear intent is especially useful while the platform is still growing its public infrastructure.

Use the site as context

The platform itself now provides enough structure to point to records, pages, and visible needs.

That context helps keep outreach grounded and productive instead of generic.
