Archive

The archive layer holds the recovered public traces that give the project density: Facebook signals, mission posts, promo fragments, interviews, community calls, and media appearances that now sit inside the structured platform instead of remaining loose evidence.

This page is designed to feel like a browseable field of traces rather than a dump. It should make clear which records are foundational, which remain partial, and how each item connects back to the wider project graph.

How to browse the archive

The archive should feel traceable, rich, and honest about uncertainty.

Some items are strong mission or media signals, some are launch-window fragments, and some still carry partial captions or manual-review flags. The page now makes those different states easier to understand at a glance.

Featured signals first

Start with the records that most clearly reveal identity, visibility, or public mobilization.

These are the entries most useful for understanding how the project appeared in the public sphere.

Source integrity stays visible

Open-source links and confidence context remain part of the interface.

The archive should feel informed and accountable, not merely atmospheric or decorative.

Manual-review cases remain honest

Partial captions and thinner recoveries are still surfaced carefully rather than hidden.

This preserves evidence truth while still making the browse layer usable and public-facing.
